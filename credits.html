Credits & sources

Data

  • Launch data: The Space Devs, Launch Library 2 API (launch times, status, holds, mission timeline, webcast links, launch-weather percentage). Used under their terms of use. They don't guarantee accuracy, and neither can we.
  • Pad weather: NOAA / National Weather Service, AviationWeather.gov METAR observations (US Government work, public domain).
  • Coastlines (map and ocean side): Natural Earth (public domain).
  • Sun position: computed with SunCalc (BSD-2-Clause).

Images and 3D

  • Sky lighting (Ultra): "Kloofendal 43d Clear (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal and "Qwantani Dusk 2 (Pure Sky)" by Greg Zaal & Jarod Guest, from Poly Haven, CC0 (public domain).
  • Rocket, launch pad, tower, cabin, clouds, smoke, exhaust and Earth are generic models and textures made in code for this site. No third-party models, photos or logos are used.
  • Sounds: every sound is generated live in your browser with the Web Audio API. No recordings are used.

Video embeds

  • Webcasts belong to their publishers and are shown with the official players: YouTube embedded player (privacy-enhanced mode) and X embedded posts. Webcast links come from Launch Library 2 or from you.

Open-source software

  • three.js (MIT License), including its Sky shader (Preetham sky model) and RGBE loader.
  • pmndrs/postprocessing (zlib License): bloom, depth of field, vignette, film grain, ACES filmic tone mapping.
  • SunCalc (BSD-2-Clause).

What's real and what's simulated

Real: launch time, status, holds and scrubs, the mission timeline, launch-weather percentage, pad weather and the sun's position. Simulated: the 3D views, the flight path, and the altitude, speed and g-force numbers (always labeled SIMULATED), plus the sound and rumble. There is no live telemetry.
